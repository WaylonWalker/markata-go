# Third-party fonts

These built-in tiers are derived from the official `google/fonts` repository
at revision `c28e08582e7bd36751febb3391142a5eb18bbb34`.

All families are licensed under OFL-1.1. The original license text is kept in
each family directory. Source outlines and naming were not intentionally
modified; the generated files are stable WOFF2 subsets produced with
FontTools 4.63.0 and Brotli support using the profiles in
`markata-fontpacks.yaml`.
